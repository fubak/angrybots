Privacy

Angry Bots is a free browser game. Leaderboards are optional.

If you sign in with X, we store your X user id, handle, display name, and avatar URL, plus your best score per level.

Your handle, name, avatar, and scores are shown publicly on the leaderboards at angrybots.lol.

Anonymous usage analytics (GA4) may be collected when enabled.

To remove everything we store, open the Leaderboard in the game and press Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Game progress (stars, achievements, settings) lives in your browser's local storage and is never sent to us except as leaderboard scores.
