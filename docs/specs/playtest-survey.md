# Playtest survey (G4 / G5)

Rate each item **1–5** (1 = poor, 5 = excellent).

1. **Fun** — How enjoyable was the session?
2. **Goal clarity** — Was it always clear what you needed to do to win?
3. **Aim control** — How well could you aim and control shot power?
4. **Destruction fairness** — Did structures and pigs react in ways that felt fair?
5. **Desire to continue** — How much do you want to play the next level?

**Open questions**

6. What did you want to do that the game wouldn't let you?
7. Anything confusing, frustrating, or broken?
